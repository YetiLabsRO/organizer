What to expect

  • Anything you write in the body of a Notion task page stays in Notion — it is yours to use freely and is never imported. Only the properties sync.
  • Deleting works both ways: deleting a task here moves its Notion page to the trash, and trashing a page in Notion deletes the task here.
  • Projects and tags typed in Notion are matched against ones that already exist here — new ones are not created, because they are shared with everyone on this server.
  • Notion only records edit times to the nearest minute. If both sides change a task within the same minute, Organizer wins.
